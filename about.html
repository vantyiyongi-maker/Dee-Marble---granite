About Dee Marble & Granite

Quality memorials created with dignity, care and respect.

Our Story

Dee Marble & Granite is a tombstone and memorial company dedicated to helping families honour the memory of their loved ones.

We understand that choosing a memorial is an important decision. Our aim is to provide families with quality marble and granite memorials that reflect the life and memory of the person being honoured.

Through careful workmanship, personalised designs and professional service, Dee Marble & Granite strives to create lasting memorials that families can cherish for generations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide families with high-quality tombstones and memorial products while delivering respectful, reliable and compassionate customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted memorial service provider known for quality craftsmanship, personalised designs and professional service.

Our Values

Quality

We are committed to producing quality memorial products and workmanship.

Respect

We treat every family and memorial decision with dignity and respect.

Integrity

We aim to provide honest and transparent services to our customers.

Personalisation

We help families create memorials that reflect the individual being remembered.

## 1. Project Overview

Dee Marble & Granite is a tombstone and memorial company that provides quality marble and granite memorial products designed to honour and preserve the memory of loved ones.

The purpose of this website is to provide the organisation with a professional online presence where customers can learn more about the company, view available products and services, submit enquiries and access the company's contact information.

The website was developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript and is designed to provide a simple, professional and user-friendly experience.

---

## 2. Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals of the Dee Marble & Granite website are:

- To create a professional online presence for the company.
- To provide customers with information about the organisation.
- To showcase available tombstones and memorial products.
- To allow potential customers to submit enquiries.
- To provide clear contact information.
- To make information easy to find through simple website navigation.
- To create a responsive and user-friendly website.
- To provide customers with an easy way to learn about the company's services before making an enquiry.

---

## 3. Target Audience

The website is primarily aimed at:

- Families looking for tombstones and memorial products.
- Individuals looking for personalised memorial solutions.
- Customers researching marble and granite tombstones.
- People who require information about tombstone designs and services.
- Customers who want to contact Dee Marble & Granite for an enquiry or quotation.
